# Quantum Builders Club: no-install notebooks

The two notebooks from the classroom's Start Here course, for running in Google Colab with nothing to install.

- [Your first quantum circuit](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Ganamedee/qbc-certificates/blob/main/notebooks/first-circuit.ipynb): one qubit, one Hadamard gate, 1,000 shots and a chart.
- [Quantum moments: your first week](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Ganamedee/qbc-certificates/blob/main/notebooks/quantum-moments.ipynb): entanglement, two H gates that cancel, and a GHZ state, for Days 2, 4 and 6.

They're copies of `course/notebooks/` in the club's repo. Change them there and copy them here, because the classroom's Open in Colab links open these.
